# Government Exam Launch Quality Standard

## Purpose

ExamGrind will not list an exam as live merely because it has a popular name.
Each launch must give a student a correct first diagnosis, a syllabus-aware
learning route, targeted practice, and a clear official source for changing
recruitment rules.

## Launch order

1. Delhi Police Constable
2. UPPSC RO/ARO
3. UP Secretariat Samiksha Adhikari / Sahayak Samiksha Adhikari
4. Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and Computer Assistant
5. UPPSC Lower PCS

UPPSC PCS is intentionally not in the first implementation wave. It requires
credible prelims, mains, answer-writing and current-affairs coverage; a small
objective-question shell would misrepresent the product.

## Required evidence before an exam goes live

- The current official notification, scheme and syllabus are linked in the
  exam guide. No coaching-page summary is treated as the source of truth.
- The exam has a reviewed subject, chapter and topic map.
- Its public diagnosis uses five original questions that test different parts
  of the real pattern, not five variations of one Quant question.
- Every diagnostic result routes to a valid subject/topic and a real next
  action in the signed-in product.
- The Coach prompt identifies the exam and does not reuse CUET/NEET advice.
- The public landing page states the exam and current scope honestly. It does
  not promise selection, a live vacancy, or coverage that has not shipped.
- A founder preview completes sign-in, diagnosis, practice, analysis, Coach
  and upgrade routes without a broken link or an incorrect exam label.

## Shared government-exam foundation

The following are shared concepts, but they remain separately mapped for each
exam because marks, language, time and eligibility vary by notification.

- General Studies and current affairs
- Reasoning
- Quantitative aptitude
- Hindi and English
- Basic computer knowledge
- State GK, when the official pattern includes it

## Content rules

- Original questions and explanations only.
- Official links for dates, eligibility and paper scheme.
- No scraped papers or answer keys.
- A question is published only if its answer, explanation and topic mapping
  are independently checked.
