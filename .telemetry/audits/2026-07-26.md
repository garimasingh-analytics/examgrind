# Tracking Audit: 2026-07-26

**Codebase:** ExamGrind  
**SDK:** GA4 `gtag.js`, Google Ads conversion, Meta Pixel

## Current Tracking Inventory

The GA4 wrapper defines 14 live events: signup, login, daily mission start, Mistake Book view/retry, quiz start/completion, mock start/completion, Deep Analysis request/completion/view, and checkout/purchase. Each wrapper is imported by a matching client component; no orphaned wrapper event was found.

## Identity Management

The browser setup does not send a GA4 `user_id`, traits, or group context. Consent is required before the tag and product events are used.

## Observed Patterns

- Event calls route through `lib/product-analytics.ts`.
- Product events are browser-side and not awaited by study or billing flows.
- Event names and properties are snake_case.
- The wrapper prevents events before explicit marketing consent.
